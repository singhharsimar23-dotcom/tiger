FraudSight: Autonomous Graph AI Investigation System

An enterprise-grade forensic investigation platform integrating TigerGraph native GSQL multi-hop graph traversals, Gemini 2.5 forensic reasoning, an information-theoretic Optimal Stopping Gate, and an Anti-Overblocking Customer Protection Filter to detect syndicated financial crime rings without penalizing legitimate customers.

1

TigerGraph Core

Native graph storage over transactions, devices, accounts, and IP gateways. Sub-second multi-hop GSQL queries uncover hidden collusion networks via derived SHARES_DEVICE and CO_LOCATED_IP edges.

2

Optimal Stopping Gate

Prevents open-ended AI looping using Value of Information (VOI) and Minimum Description Length (MDL) principles. Halts investigation when marginal evidence gain is insufficient to alter the final risk decision.

3

Customer Protection Filter

Protects legitimate customers by symmetrically checking innocence archetypes (recurring subscriptions, airline flight velocity corridors, registered hardware) before executing account blocks.

folder_zip Institutional Output Dossier Standard

Every completed benchmark investigation produces 4 canonical forensic output artifacts saved under outputs/cases/case_XX/:

  • case_record.json: Complete investigation ledger, evidence weights, MDL scores, and multi-turn decision log.
  • sar.json: Suspicious Activity Report formatted to FinCEN Form 111 / FFIEC regulatory specifications.
  • action_before.json: Pre-investigation baseline policy posture (initial triage status).
  • action_after.json: Post-investigation institutional action, approval queue, and supervisor signoff.
